AIP, the American Independence Project, is an independent advocacy effort with one premise: American policy should be decided by Americans, for Americans. Nobody else gets a vote, and nobody else should get to buy one.

What we do

We track the money foreign-interest lobbies put into Congress, starting with the largest and most effective one, the Israel lobby, and we track the money Congress sends back out as foreign aid. We turn public records into content people actually see: a searchable database, sourced arguments, and shareable graphics.

What we want

End foreign aid packages. Register and disclose all foreign-interest lobbying under FARA. Stop campaign spending built to serve any foreign government's agenda, whichever government it is. Money spent to steer US policy should be visible to every voter.

How we work

Primary sources only: FEC filings, Congressional Research Service reports, government documents, and on-the-record statements. Every number on this site links to where it came from. When we're wrong, we say so and fix it publicly. We're partisan about America, not about party; our database covers both, and the money does too.

What we're not

We challenge policy, money, and institutions, never a people or a faith. Bigotry, including antisemitism, is both wrong and a gift to the lobbies we criticize, because it lets them change the subject from the receipts. If that's what you're here for, you're on the wrong site.

Why "the Israel lobby" first?

Because it's the biggest test case, by its own numbers. No other foreign-aligned lobby spends $100 million in a single election cycle, and no other country receives a guaranteed $3.8 billion a year from US taxpayers. If Americans can't have an honest, sourced conversation about the largest example, the principle means nothing. The same standard applies to every country: no foreign aid, no foreign influence, no exceptions.
