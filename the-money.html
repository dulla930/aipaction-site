Methodology & source

Figures are career totals from pro-Israel PACs and their large donors: direct and earmarked PAC donations to candidates, plus independent expenditures (ads, mailers, organizing) made on their behalf. Data is compiled from Federal Election Commission filings, as of July 5, 2026. "$0 — refuses lobby $" marks members with no pro-Israel PAC money on record. "Not listed" means no total is published yet for that member. All 50 state delegations are covered. Spot an error? Email us with the filing and we'll fix it within 24 hours.
